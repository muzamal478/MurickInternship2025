# Marketing Recommendations by Segment

## Champions (Low Recency, High Frequency/Monetary)
- **Strategy**: Reward & engage with VIP perks, early access.
- **Tactics**: Personalized emails, loyalty points (20% off).

## Loyal Customers (Medium RFM)
- **Strategy**: Upsell & cross-sell.
- **Tactics**: Product bundles, referral bonuses.

## At-Risk (High Recency, Medium FM)
- **Strategy**: Reactivation campaigns.
- **Tactics**: Discount codes, feedback surveys.

## Lost Customers (High Recency, Low FM)
- **Strategy**: Win-back offers.
- **Tactics**: 30% off first purchase, re-engagement emails.

**Expected ROI**: 25% increase in retention, 15% revenue lift.
