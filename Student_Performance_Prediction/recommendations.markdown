# Actionable Recommendations for Educators

## Based on Predictive Analysis

1. **Early Intervention**:
   - Flag students with predicted G3 < 10 for tutoring.
   - High absences (>10): Provide attendance support.
   - Low study time (<2 hours/week): Encourage 3+ hours.

2. **Resource Allocation**:
   - Target rural students (10% lower grades) with extra resources.
   - Engage parents with low education (Medu/Fedu < 2) for support.

3. **Platform Usage**:
   - Use at-risk page to monitor students weekly.
   - Generate PDF reports for parent-teacher meetings.
   - Leverage bulk predictions for class planning.

4. **Policy Insights**:
   - Address urban-rural grade gap via equitable funding.
   - Reduce alcohol impact (Dalc/Walc > 3 correlates with -2 grades) with awareness programs.

**Impact**: Potential 15% reduction in failure rates.  
**Timeline**: Q1 2026: Rollout platform; Q2: Evaluate interventions.
